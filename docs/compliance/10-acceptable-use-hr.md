# Acceptable use and people

People with production access (GitHub, AWS, Atlas, Hostinger, Stripe) must use unique accounts and MFA. Sharing passwords or admin cookies is an incident.

Joiner: named account, least privilege, recorded on the access sheet.  
Leaver: same-day revoke.  
Awareness: annual read of policies `01`–`06` (checkbox on the access review).

Background checks and formal security training certificates are **company HR** items for the auditor — not stored in this repo.
